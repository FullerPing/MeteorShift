# Pet model conventions

The art brief and rarity ladder are in [pets.md](pets.md). `tools/build-pet-models.luau` builds all fourteen saved models deterministically in Studio Edit mode. Execute the complete file, inspect the models, save the place without publishing, and export `ReplicatedStorage.PetModels` to `assets/PetModels.rbxm`. The tool validates the complete staged folder before replacing the existing one.

Each model uses the pet id from `Config.Pets` as its name. Its `PrimaryPart` is `Body`, and its `PetColor` attribute is the configured colour. `Level10` holds complete replacement geometry, including another `Body`; the client promotes that folder's contents at level 10 and removes the inactive geometry. Both looks count toward the authoring limit: fewer than 300 BaseParts, or at most 400 for Legendary and Premium. Stored parts are unanchored, massless, noncolliding, nontouchable and nonqueryable. Models contain no scripts. Lights and particle emitters begin at Rare. Only Solar Phoenix has a halo.

`Head`, `Tail`, `Wings`, `Drill`, `Halo`, and related parts retain useful names. `AnimationGroup` and `AnimationPivot` group articulated pieces around a local joint without scripts or physics constraints. `Flying` chooses hovering instead of a ground trot; `WorkingStyle` selects the brief's work gesture. `Pannier` parts grow at level 5 before the full level-10 replacement. Trails use attachments, and emitters marked `WorkingBurst` fire on validated perk events.

The server publishes matching comma-separated `Pets` and `PetLevels` Player attributes and the available `PetSlots`. `PetVisualController` creates anchored local clones in `Workspace.LocalPetVisuals`, tints `Body`, and uses smoothed `PivotTo` movement. The folder is created on each client and survives camera replacement. Slots 1 and 2 follow behind-left and behind-right; slots 3 and 4 perch above the shoulders. Selection prioritizes the local player, then distance, culls beyond 150 studs, and never permits more than forty pet models, reserving one for a hatch preview. Missing models or bodies are silently skipped, and unequips, character removal and player departure destroy followers.

`PetService.Working(userId, role, targetPosition?)` is a cosmetic server event sent after real work; the client animates equipped pets carrying that perk. Mining gestures use the validated target position, and hauling gestures locate the owner's visible hopper or shared refinery. `PetBackpackFraction` controls the Golem's near-full hatch for all owners. Work chips, shards, flame, ore and sunlight are authored parts hidden by the clone helper until animated; they do not create extra models. Quiet local pet sounds reuse the game's existing approved audio sources. The Sparrow's sale chirp remains cosmetic alongside its Scholar perk.

`ReduceEffects` disables lights, particles, trails, pet sounds, wobble and ornamental movement while preserving followers and readable results. A hatch wobbles for 1.5 seconds, flashes its rarity colour, then shows the name, current perks and duplicate XP. `HatchOpen` releases gameplay input at two seconds; the result stays readable until dismissed or 4.5 seconds, with queued hatches separated by an input-free gap. Escape, gamepad B, the backdrop and the 44-pixel continue button dismiss it. `HatchController` reserves its preview synchronously through `PetVisualController:ReserveHatchPreview(true)` before creating geometry, then destroys the preview before releasing that reservation, including when reduced effects skip the wobble.
