# Map v2: cartoon-simulator hub

The live `Workspace.Map` since 8 October 2026, re-laid out on 8 October (evening) after the owner's reference screenshot. Same 540 × 540 play area, same crater, same gameplay contracts; new layout, new look, new surroundings. The models were rebuilt in a cartoon-simulator style on the same layout on 8 October (late): rounded, smooth, three-tone colour ramps (`Toon.luau`). (Folder and stage names still say "Main Street" in places; there is no boulevard any more.)

## Layout (`src/shared/WorldGeometry.luau` is the source of truth)
Screen conventions: the owner's reference is a top-down view with the spawn at the bottom, so screen-right is world −X.
- **South plaza:** a cream apron 80 wide runs from just behind the **Refinery** (0, −177) north to the ring road. The Refinery faces north (front and UsePoint (0, 3, −158.5) toward the crater). The six spawn points are invisible, non-colliding SpawnLocations between its front and the ring road (rows z −153 and −148), turned to face north, so players spawn in front of the Refinery looking at the meteor. There is no spawn plaza, pad or METEORSHIFT sign.
- **Shops angled toward the crater, just outside the ring:** Gear Hall SE (112, −145), Hatchery SW (−112, −145), each with its own forecourt apron to the ring (zones carry `facing` and `lat` × `dep`; `width` × `depth` bound the rotated rectangle). Trading Post west (−176, −100), Rebirth east (176, −100); the town fountain stands by the spawn plaza (100, −236).
- **Ore fields:** Rust Works NW (−190, 185), Frost Yard NE (190, 185), Violet Quarry W (−214, 62), Sulfur Ridge E (214, 62). Diagonal spokes serve the north fields, straight spokes the side fields.
- **North:** the Meteor Watch observatory at the end of the north spoke (0, 172), the first-meteor statue (−68, 222) and a 1-2-3 "TOP MINERS" podium with a trophy (75, 230). The Mineral Index has no building: it is the HUD Index button.
- **Crater Isle (outer scenery, 9 October):** the town sits on an island inside a castle rampart (`Boundary`: solid studded walls inner face ±270, a dark base course, pillar strips with banners, a two-tier cap, 16 rounded merlons per side, lanterns, four big round corner towers and four closed decorative gatehouses, whose gate arches are flat reliefs). Behind it (`Surroundings`): a cliff skirt, a sand beach and foam ring, a belt of tree-topped hills whose crowns peek over the merlons, a turquoise lagoon, palm islands, two lighthouses (south-east and south-west) and sailboats. Far away (`Backdrop`): a middle range of teal mountains, a far range of snow-capped violet peaks, **Meteor Peak** (a volcano with a glowing lava lake, neon crystals and a smoke plume straight behind the north gate), fallen meteor rocks, three floating sky islands with waterfalls and soft clouds. Outer scenery never collides (only the four walls and the kill plane do), is query-free, and `Surroundings`/`Backdrop` are `ModelStreamingMode.Persistent` so the far layers stream in for every player.

## Authoring
These are Edit-time commands outside the Rojo tree. They create anchored art and world SurfaceGuis; no saved scripts. The world is authored **in place** as `Workspace.Map_Redesign` at offset zero (`WorldGeometry.Settings.StageOffset` is `Vector3.zero`) and renamed `Map` at the swap, so nothing is shifted and no WorldPivot can drift.

1. Serve the tools folder: `python -m http.server 8123 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory tools`.
2. Stop Play, then run from Studio MCP `execute_luau` (`datamodel_type = "Edit"`):
   ```luau
   local H = game:GetService("HttpService")
   local function L(n, ...) return assert(loadstring(H:GetAsync("http://127.0.0.1:8123/map-v2/" .. n .. ".luau", true), n))(...) end
   L("Common"); L("Brick"); L("Toon")
   return L("Roads")
   ```
3. Order for a full rebuild: `Crater` → `Roads` → `Refinery` → `GearHall` → `Hatchery` → `TradingPost` → `Rebirth` → `DebrisFields` → `Boundary` → `Surroundings` → `Backdrop` → `Scenery` → `StudStyle` (alone) → `StaticQA`. Each stage owns only its named groups and refuses to replace unowned ones.
4. Every stage after `Roads` needs `Toon` loaded. The Refinery no longer clones the v1 station models.
5. `Brick.luau` is the shared grammar: `A.frameFor(zone, facing)` gives a building its own frame (lateral `u`, depth `d` toward the front), with `A.box`, `A.stud`, `A.studGrid`, `A.awning`, `A.signOn`, `A.pillars`.
6. `Toon.luau` (`A.toon`) is the cartoon grammar on top of it: colour ramps `T.ramp.<name> = {light, body, dark}`, shapes (`T.box`, `T.cyl`, `T.cylLat`, `T.cylDep`, `T.ball`, `T.wedge`, `T.gable`, `T.dome`, `T.blob` for one-part ellipsoid hills), wall details (`T.window`, `T.door`, `T.steps`), props (`T.lamp`, `T.crate`, `T.barrel`, `T.banner`, `T.planter`, `T.tree`) and `T.mesh(parent, name, cf, height, fallback)`, which clones a hero mesh from `ServerStorage.MapArt` when present and otherwise builds the parts fallback. `Anvil`, `GiantEgg`, `Trophy` and the three pedestal eggs `Egg_<EggId>` are stored (Blender meshes, `tools/blender-assets/`); with them `StaticQA` counts 1,941 parts and 18 MeshParts.

7. **Biomes (10 October 2026)**, after the owner's four-worlds picture: `Biomes.luau` turns each field's corner into a biome quarter (Rust Works, Frost Yard, Violet Quarry, Sulfur Ridge). It owns `Map.Biomes`: ground rows in the biome's light tone between the grass and the lawn tiles (clipped to the ring road at radius 134), the `LawnTile`s inside a quarter recoloured to its dark tone (original colour kept in attr `BiomeOriginalColor`), and decoration props placed by seeded rejection sampling only on open grass (clear of zones, the 150-stud landing circle and every queryable map part). Props: ice crystals, snowy pines, snow mounds and ice cliffs; rust mesas, derricks and ore piles; amethyst clusters and dark spires; golden mesas and glowing pools. Leaves of `Scenery` trees and bushes inside a quarter take its palette. Mineable rocks, field floors, roads, buildings and the crater are untouched. The live map is `Workspace.Map` while `Common` authors `Map_Redesign`, so rename it for the run:
   ```luau
   local map = workspace.Map; map.Name = "Map_Redesign"
   local ok, r = pcall(function() L("Common"); L("Brick"); L("Toon"); return L("Biomes") end)
   map.Name = "Map"; assert(ok, r); return r
   ```
   **Quarters (10 October, later):** the four areas are now slices of the circle (`WorldGeometry.Settings.Quarters`, angles round the crater; the town keeps the south). Each slice has a gate on its centre line just past the ring road, a field road to the ORE landing spot, a fan of 20 rocks opening toward the wall (`Settings.Fan`; 80 rocks in all) and the field's landmark at the far end. Biomes decorates the rest of the slice: striped spoke ground, then the same prop set per quarter (big ones near the walls, trees, medium, small, boulders). The side quarters have less room than the corner ones, so they may get a few fewer props. The field zones in `WorldGeometry` are now computed boxes round each gate, fan and landmark. Order: Roads, remove `Map.Biomes`, DebrisFields (it also removes scenery standing on fans, landmarks and field roads), Biomes, StaticQA. The fans were widened about 30% (HalfStart 31, Spread 0.97; slots 20 apart) and the ground spokes sit on four heights .07 apart, so overlapping neighbours never flicker. Result: 2,232 inner parts, StaticQA 84 routes with 0 failures.
8. **Commissary (10 October 2026):** `Commissary.luau` replaces the Meteor Watch observatory (zone renamed `Commissary`) with a small Foundry outpost facing the crater. It owns `TownSquare.Commissary` with its own `UsePoint`; `WorldMap.refineryPoints` returns both points, `canUseRefinery` accepts either and `RefineryService` hangs the same prompt on each. Scenery no longer builds the observatory (the statue and podium stay in `Scenery.MeteorWatch`). StaticQA routes every spawn to the Commissary too: 90 routes.
9. **Leaderboards (10 October 2026):** `Leaderboards.luau` puts two boards on the south plaza either side of the Refinery (zones `LeaderboardEast` = TOP MINERS / bars sold, `LeaderboardWest` = TOP REBIRTHS), turned 25 degrees toward the spawn, and clears scenery standing on them. It owns `TownSquare.Leaderboards.<Id>` with a `Screen` part; `LeaderboardService` builds the rows on it and fills them from `Config.Leaderboards` (OrderedDataStores, or this server's players when DataStores are unavailable).

## Style rules
- Smooth surfaces everywhere except the ground, roads, crater and the four boundary walls (`StudStyle` textures only those).
- One main colour per building in its three tones, white or cream trim, `T.ink` for signs and details; Neon only for glow accents.
- Each building has a hero object on its roof (crossed pickaxes, giant egg, gold coin, floating star, chimney) and props at its door.
- Debris rocks are a root plus `RockChunk` parts (shape only, never tinted) and `OreFleck` parts (tinted by tier at runtime). `DebrisService` tints only `OreFleck` children; `Config.Debris.Rock.Studs = false` keeps rocks smooth.
- Budgets: inner map 2,700 parts (raised from 2,200 on 10 October 2026), outer scenery (`Boundary` + `Surroundings` + `Backdrop`) 1,400 parts (currently 1,257: 521 / 444 / 292), at most 24 MeshParts (currently 14), 12 enabled lights (currently 6), 16 emitters (currently 7). `A.innerCount` (Common) counts the inner map; `StaticQA` checks both, that the outer scenery does not collide, does not sit inside the play area, and that `Surroundings`/`Backdrop` are Persistent.
- Outer scenery helpers in `Toon.luau`: `T.deco`/`T.far` (non-colliding parts), `T.persistent`, `T.polar`, `T.lollipop`, `T.peak`, `T.cloud`, `T.roundTower`. Rampart parts must not have names ending in `Wall` (`StudStyle` studs only the four solid walls).
- Atmosphere (set in the place, not in git): Density 0.22, Offset 0.25, Haze 1.0, Color (200,224,255), Decay (120,150,220), a lavender distance for the far ranges (before: 0.17, 0.10, 0.5, (177,225,255), (153,191,235)).

To restore an older map: move `Map` out, move `Map_v3` (or `Map_v2`, `Map_v1`) into Workspace and rename it `Map`. The `WorldGeometry.luau` zone table must be restored to match (v1: commit `4ad9484`; the Main Street zones were never committed, so commit before the next big change if you want to keep them). Delete `SwapRollback` once the published game is confirmed.
